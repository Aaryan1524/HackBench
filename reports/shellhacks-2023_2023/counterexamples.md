# Counterexamples & Anti-Bias Audits

Automated sanity checks challenging popular hackathon heuristics.

## Hypothesis: "AI-heavy projects possess an inherent winning advantage."
- **Finding**: AI was present in 42% of winners versus 28% of non-winners. Given the high overall base rate (31%), AI is essentially table stakes rather than a standalone differentiator.
- **Base Rate Overall**: 31% (72/232)
- **Prevalence Among Winners**: 42% (16/38)
- **Prevalence Among Non Winners**: 28% (56/194)

---
## Hypothesis: "The most technically complex codebase wins."
- **Finding**: Massive codebases do not guarantee victory. Simpler projects with focused workflows and polished presentations routinely triumph over sprawling engineering efforts.
- **Counterexample Simplest Winner**: Find Your Paradise won with only ~0 verified LOC.
- **Counterexample Complex Non Winner**: Save Right did not win despite building ~535886 verified LOC.

---
## Hypothesis: "A pre-recorded video demo is mandatory to win."
- **Finding**: 25/38 winning projects won without any pre-recorded video demo on Devpost, proving in-person live expo demonstrations and pitches were decisive.
- **Counterexamples Found**: 25
- **Examples**: ['IntelliSchedule', 'MedVoyage', 'Melorize']
